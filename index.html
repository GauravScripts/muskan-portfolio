I help large organisations move climate and ESG out of the sustainability report and into the risk register, the capex plan and the boardroom.

Over five years at Deloitte, KPMG and PwC I have led discrete workstreams for Fortune 500 clients across automotive, BFSI and industrial sectors: double materiality under CSRD, NGFS climate scenario modelling, EU Taxonomy alignment, financed emissions under PCAF, and readiness for EUDR, PPWR and IFRS S1/S2.

What sets the work apart is the tooling. I build the VBA scoring engines, Python emission-factor databases and Power BI heatmaps that turn a one-off assessment into a repeatable practice asset, so analysts spend their hours on advisory instead of arithmetic. That approach has cut delivery timelines by up to 67%, halved GHG calculation time and been adopted as team standards.

I also coach three analysts per engagement, own QA protocols for GHG inventories and disclosures, and designed an eight-module ESG capability programme for 50+ consultants. Outside client work I write on climate risk, nature and disclosure, and judge student sustainability case competitions.

How I work

  • Start from the client’s own numbers, then layer climate drivers on top. Quantify before you narrate.
  • Build the tool once. Reusable IP beats heroic spreadsheets, and it scales the whole practice.
  • Coach as you deliver. QA protocols and case-study IP lift first-pass accuracy for everyone on the team.
  • Write for the board. Heatmaps, risk registers and roadmaps a CFO can fund, not just a sustainability team can admire.
